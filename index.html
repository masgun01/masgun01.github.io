School enrolment system

Online Enrolment for SLB Sana Dharma

Problem

Enrolment was handled on paper. Parents had to travel to the school with their documents, and staff checked and tallied every application by hand.

What I built

An online enrolment system for a special-needs school covering three levels of schooling. Parents apply from home and staff verify each application from an admin panel.

Features

  • Account sign-up with email activation
  • Student and parent/guardian forms
  • Document upload, reviewed file by file
  • Results announcement and re-registration
  • Email notification at every stage
  • Live admin notifications over WebSocket
  • Applicant data exported to Excel and PDF
  • Reports and a user activity log

Data and forecasting

Coffee Stock Planning System

Problem

A packaged-coffee producer needed to know how much raw material to prepare for the coming month. Estimates made by hand were often wrong, leaving them short or overstocked.

What I built

A web app that forecasts sales with a Random Forest model and converts the result into roasted bean and green bean requirements.

Features

  • Sales data upload with automatic cleaning
  • Product mapping to variants and SKU codes
  • Next-month forecast with a prediction interval
  • Stock plan with service-level safety stock
  • Monthly forecast accuracy monitoring
  • Stock plan downloadable as Excel
  • Runs on a laptop (MySQL) and online (PostgreSQL)
  • Data migration and backup scripts

How I work

The same six steps on every project, so you always know what happens next.

  1. DiscussionYou describe what you need. I ask whatever has to be settled first. No charge.
  2. Written quoteFeature list, price, timeline, and number of revisions, all sent before any payment.
  3. DepositWork starts once the deposit arrives. The balance is due when you have approved the result.
  4. BuildI send progress updates as I go, so you can look and redirect early rather than at the end.
  5. RevisionsChanges are made within the number of revisions agreed in the quote.
  6. HandoverThe site goes live. You receive the source code, admin accounts, and a usage guide.
