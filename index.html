Online Enrolment for SLB Sana Dharma

problem

Enrolment was handled on paper. Parents travelled to the school with their documents, and staff checked and tallied every application by hand.

what I built

An online enrolment system for a special-needs school covering three levels. Parents apply from home and staff verify each application from an admin panel.

  • Account sign-up with email activation
  • Student and parent/guardian forms
  • Document upload, reviewed file by file
  • Results announcement and re-registration
  • Email notification at every stage
  • Live admin notifications over WebSocket
  • Applicant data exported to Excel and PDF
  • Reports and a user activity log

02 / 05Data & forecasting

Coffee Stock Planning System

problem

A packaged-coffee producer needed to know how much raw material to prepare each month. Estimates made by hand were often off, leaving them short or overstocked.

what I built

A web app that forecasts sales with a Random Forest model and converts the result into roasted bean and green bean requirements.

  • Sales data upload with automatic cleaning
  • Product mapping to variants and SKU codes
  • Next-month forecast with a prediction interval
  • Stock plan with service-level safety stock
  • Monthly forecast accuracy monitoring
  • Stock plan downloadable as Excel
  • Runs on a laptop (MySQL) and online (PostgreSQL)
  • Data migration and backup scripts
